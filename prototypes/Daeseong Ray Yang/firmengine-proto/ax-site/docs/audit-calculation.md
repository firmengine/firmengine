# Calculation and output adversarial audit

Scope: `lib/domain.ts`, `lib/assessment.ts`, `lib/exports.ts`, `lib/form-mapping.ts`, and `lib/office-writer.ts`. All newly added cases are synthetic. No user sample, gold, or holdout data was opened, and no model call was made.

## Reproduced and corrected

| Area | Adversarial input | Prior behavior | Correction |
|---|---|---|---|
| Income aggregation | A business source reports `-100,000 KRW/month` net while other sources are positive | The negative source reduced the combined monthly income, implicitly offsetting wages without any source-level review | `monthlyIncome` now blocks a negative source amount with `INCOME_NET_NEGATIVE_REQUIRES_REVIEW`; the assessment explains that the loss must be classified and reviewed before it can affect the combined amount |
| Repeated payroll / annual evidence | Three same-payer monthly payslips or two years of same-business annual income certificates arrive as separate `IncomeSource` rows | Each row's monthly amount was summed as if it were an independent concurrent income, overstating observed monthly income by three times or two times | Same source-backed payer ID and income type are grouped as time observations. Nonoverlapping full-month ranges are duration-weighted averaged; annual periods use a complete calendar-month denominator inferred from start/end dates. `filingPeriod` excludes out-of-window periods. The output exposes per-source values and `incomeGroups` separately |
| Same-period bonus / duplicate ambiguity | Same payer and period has two different source documents or amounts | Automatic summing could double-count a repeated statement; automatic dedup could erase a bonus or second payroll component | Overlapping values now block that group's monthly result unless an explicit human `aggregationTreatment` plus review reason identifies a duplicate, independent source, or same-period component. Exact same source references are deduplicated safely |
| Missing representative period | Source-linked observations exist without a selected legal representative window, or evidence covers only part of it | A numeric observed average could appear to be a legally selected current income | The engine still reports the evidence-window average, but marks it provisional and adds `INCOME_REPRESENTATIVE_PERIOD_NOT_SELECTED` or coverage findings. A selected period has a separate strict-boolean `incomePeriodReviewed` state; the UI must expose it for completion |
| Proposal fallback | A negative/non-normalizable approved monthly payment is explicitly present while disposable income is positive | A null result from nonnegative validation was treated like an absent value and silently replaced with calculated disposable income | Explicit invalid proposals now produce `APPROVED_PAYMENT_INVALID` and remain null. Only a genuinely absent proposal may use positive disposable income as a provisional default |
| Present value | An exact present-value amount and any nonempty free-text explanation are entered, but the method/date has not been reviewed | The liquidation comparison could be treated as a calculated trace based on the asset-valuation review flag alone | Added a separate strict-boolean `presentValueReviewed` state. Present value remains distinct from nominal total; comparison is provisional and finalization is blocked until its own review is recorded |
| Guarantee / joint claim | A guarantee entry links to an underlying principal debt and both are treated as unsecured fixed claims | The statutory debt-cap bucket could count overlapping claims without considering guarantee role, subrogation or whether the guarantee amount is only a contract limit | Added explicit guarantee metadata and human-only `capTreatment`; linked underlying claims remain cap-unknown unless one side is expressly excluded after review. Source-stated guarantee amount/limit are displayed separately from applicant principal/interest and never feed arithmetic. AI extraction forcibly resets cap treatment to unknown |
| Reviewed fact warnings | A human verifies a source fact previously tagged `USER_EDIT` or uncertain | The prior warning could remain a blocking validation issue even after verification | A verified fact version resolves only designated reading/edit warnings; unresolved `FIELD_CONFLICT`/unit issues remain. The prior version retains the original warnings as history |
| Explicit zero vs absent | Zero and missing interest are given as distinct values | Existing representation can preserve both states | Added regression coverage confirming `0` remains exact while missing remains null |
| Fractional / invalid periods | Monthly amount with conflicting month count, missing or non-integer denominators | Existing deterministic period checks reject invalid denominators and conflicts | Existing checks retained; audit regression suite is being expanded without rounding unsupported inputs |

## Material limits still requiring product/legal decisions

- Asset ownership value uses ceiling division in KRW. This avoids understating a fractional share, but can overstate by one won for sub-won proportional results. No reviewed official rule specifying the rounding convention was located in this audit, so the convention should remain explicit and receive legal/accounting approval before it is treated as a filing value.
- The present-value comparison intentionally does not derive a discount rate or substitute nominal installments. It relies on a reviewed present-value amount and a free-text basis. A reviewer must establish the method, rate, and valuation date; no unverified default rate is embedded.
- Payer identity must be grounded in an identifier such as the employer's business number. An employer name alone is insufficient to merge separate businesses with the same name or conclude that identical-period rows are copies. The user-facing flow needs to expose the source-backed payer ID, representative-period selection/review, and the human aggregation treatment/reason choices. The calculation emits blocked/provisional states if those choices are absent.
- A creditor's `legalReview: rejected` does not itself exclude the debt's arithmetic amount. That status may mean the proposed legal classification was rejected while the amount remains a valid exposure. A distinct “claim amount excluded” decision and reason would be needed before dropping an amount from all aggregates.
- Guarantee, joint, future, and conditional claims now have a typed review boundary, but the field mapping and human-entry UI still need to be wired through the application. The engine preserves each asserted creditor claim in exposure totals; cap inclusion waits for explicit human treatment, and a linked principal/guarantee pair cannot be counted twice without a reviewed exclusion. This is a conservative implementation boundary, not a final ruling on how a court will treat every guarantee.

## Legal reference checked

The official Korean statute text for Debtor Rehabilitation and Bankruptcy Act Article 614 states that the total value of repayment on individual rehabilitation claims, valued as of confirmation of the repayment plan, must be at least the amount distributable in bankruptcy, subject to the statutory creditor-consent exception. The Supreme Court's 2013 decision also discusses repayment present value separately and emphasizes review of income continuity, expenses and assets; it does not prescribe a universal discount rate. Article 581 defines individual-rehabilitation claims by pre-commencement financial claims, but these sources do not establish a universal guarantee-overlap formula. See [Article 614](https://law.go.kr/lsLinkCommonInfo.do?chrClsCd=010202&lsJoLnkSeq=1024710759), [Article 581](https://www.law.go.kr/LSW/lsSideInfoP.do?docCls=jo&joBrNo=00&joNo=0581&lsiSeq=290631&urlMode=lsScJoRltInfoR), and [Supreme Court decision 2013Ma101](https://www.law.go.kr/LSW/precInfoP.do?precSeq=171784).

## Validation

Run `node --test --experimental-strip-types tests/*.test.ts` from the project directory. New income tests use synthetic payroll amounts, periods, payers and source facts only. Passing tests validate arithmetic and ambiguity gates, not the legal sufficiency of an actual filing or OCR accuracy targets.

## Integration follow-up

Income identity and aggregation-review fields are connected to the separate data CRUD page; the calculation/context page exposes the representative-period start/end and explicit review. Clearing a period clears its saved value and review. Editing or re-extracting income invalidates prior period review. Source facts and review decisions remain separate.

A second arithmetic audit caught remainder loss when annual sums were rounded to monthly amounts before weighted averaging. Group totals now sum the original period amounts and divide once; 11,999 + 12,001 over 24 months yields exactly 1,000 won, rather than 999. Component rows count the union of covered months so two January components cannot stand in for missing February evidence. Public-form business income now uses grouped amounts instead of summing raw period observations. DOCX and workbook include both observation rows and period-group aggregates.
