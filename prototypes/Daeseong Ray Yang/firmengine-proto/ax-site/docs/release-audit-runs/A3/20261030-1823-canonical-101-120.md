## 3. Reproduced P1: source-only business verification false positives

Original main code blob for lib/business-source-evidence.ts: 0b796d9c3bc608c1224c5f9a049c80b6951a8b74.
BusinessSourceMoneyRow.verifiedAndAcceptedSource checked reviewed/verified, source refs, validation and numeric.amountWon but did not compare current Fact.value with Fact.rawValue and numeric.rawValue, unit against numeric.unit, or fieldObservation.sourceState=present. Audit.amountWon could thus show an old normalized value while a corrected raw source, missing observation or conflict was marked verified. In contrast business-net-reconciliation preflight has a stronger money() guard. This is an audit trust-flag discrepancy, not proof of a successful court filing or completed income calculation.
